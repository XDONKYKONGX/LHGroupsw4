Quality Building and Maintenance Solutions

At LH Group SW Ltd, we provide comprehensive building and maintenance services that combine technical expertise with exceptional craftsmanship. From small renovations to complete home builds, we deliver quality results that stand the test of time.

Our experienced team handles every aspect of construction with precision and attention to detail, ensuring your project is completed to the highest standards.

New Build and Development

Realize your dream home with our complete home building service. We manage the entire process from initial design to final finishing touches.

Our team works closely with you to create a custom home that reflects your lifestyle and preferences, ensuring every detail meets your expectations.

General Building

We are your trusted partner for construction projects of any scale, from initial concept to final walkthrough.

Our meticulous approach ensures that every project meets the highest standards of safety and durability, delivering code-compliant results that provide lasting value and peace of mind for years to come.

Extensions

Expand your living space with a beautifully designed extension that seamlessly integrates with your existing property. We handle everything from planning permission to completion.

Whether you need extra bedroom space, a larger kitchen, or a sunroom, our team will create a functional and attractive extension that adds value to your home.

Loft Conversions

Transforming your loft requires expertly building the internal framework that defines its new rooms. We construct sturdy partition walls, dormer cheek walls, and gable ends that are perfectly integrated with your existing structure.

Our services include building new walls to create bedrooms or bathrooms, installing insulated exterior walls for dormers, and crafting elegant feature walls that maximize space and light in your unique new living area.

Expert Home Renovations

We transform your existing property into the home of your dreams, whether you're updating a single room or undertaking a full-scale remodel. Our process is built on clear communication, meticulous planning, and a deep understanding of structural integrity and modern design.

Our services range from kitchen and bathroom upgrades to room additions, open-plan conversions, and whole-house refurbishments. We manage every detail to minimize disruption and deliver a finished product that is both beautiful and built to last.

Bespoke Bathroom & Tiling

Transform your bathroom into a personal sanctuary with our custom renovation services. We focus on blending optimal layout with luxurious fixtures and thoughtful lighting to create a space that is both practical and a pleasure to use.

The perfect tiling is the cornerstone of a stunning bathroom. We specialize in intricate patterns, feature walls, and premium material selection, executing flawless installations that turn your vision into a waterproof and easy-to-maintain reality.

Professional Hard Landscaping Services

We provide durable and attractive hard landscaping solutions to structure and enhance your outdoor area. Our work forms the solid framework of your garden, ensuring it is both usable and visually appealing throughout the year.

Our services include the installation of patios, walkways, driveways, and retaining walls, as well as the construction of decorative features like pergola bases and steps. We focus on quality materials, precise installation, and excellent drainage for long-lasting results.

Conservatories & Orangeries

Beautifully blend indoor comfort with the beauty of your garden in a bright, versatile living space. Whether you desire the panoramic views of a conservatory or the solid elegance of an orangery, we create a stunning room that floods your home with natural light.

Our bespoke designs are tailored to your property's architecture and your lifestyle, from a light-filled family room to an elegant entertainment space. Using high-performance glass and materials, we ensure your new extension is a comfortable, energy-efficient retreat for all seasons.

Complete Property Maintenance

We provide a single, reliable solution for all your domestic and commercial property upkeep needs. From routine inspections and minor repairs to emergency call-outs, our proactive service ensures your property remains safe, functional, and well-presented throughout the year.

Our extensive service portfolio includes plumbing, electrical, decorating, carpentry, and grounds maintenance. By partnering with us, you benefit from scheduled preventative care that protects your asset's value and minimizes the risk of costly, disruptive emergencies.

Skilled Labour Subcontracting

We provide reliable, skilled labour to support your construction projects and meet critical deadlines. Our experienced teams are proficient in all phases of on-site work, seamlessly integrating with your existing operations and management structure.

From groundwork and framing to finishing touches, we undertake all site tasks according to your specifications and schedules. We supply the workforce and tools necessary to get the job done efficiently, safely, and to the highest standard of quality.

Professional Project Management

We take full responsibility for your construction project, ensuring it is delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards. Our meticulous approach coordinates every detail, from initial planning and supplier liaison to scheduling and on-site supervision.

As your single point of contact, we manage all trades, timelines, and logistics, mitigating risks and solving problems proactively. This comprehensive oversight removes the stress from you, providing clarity, control, and confidence throughout the entire build process.
