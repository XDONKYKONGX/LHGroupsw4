GRP Fabrication & Pattern Making Experts

We specialise in the complete GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic) fabrication process, from initial concept and master pattern creation to the production of high-quality, durable components. Our expert pattern making is the critical first step, ensuring every mould and final product meets precise specifications for complex shapes and custom designs.

Using advanced techniques and materials, we fabricate strong, lightweight, and consistent parts for a wide range of industries. Whether you need a single prototype or a full production run, our end-to-end service guarantees a perfect finish and dimensional accuracy every time.

GRP Roofing with a 25-Year Guarantee

Invest in long-term peace of mind with our Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP) roofing systems, backed by a robust 25-year guarantee. This seamless, fibreglass solution creates a incredibly durable, watertight membrane that is resistant to weathering, cracks, and UV degradation, ideal for flat roofs, dormers, and porches.

Our expert installation ensures a perfect, monolithic finish with no seams or joints, eliminating the most common points of failure. Combined with our industry-leading guarantee, this provides a virtually maintenance-free roofing solution that protects your property for decades to come.

Balconies & Valley Gutters

We design and construct robust, weatherproof balconies that create valuable outdoor space while ensuring structural integrity and safety. Our expertise extends to the installation of precision-formed valley gutters, a critical roofing component that provides reliable, discreet water management at the intersection of roof planes.

Using materials like galvanised steel, aluminium, and GRP, we fabricate solutions that are built to last. For balconies, we focus on safe access and drainage, while our valley gutters are engineered to handle high-volume rainfall, protecting the building's fabric from water damage for years to come.

GRP Walkways

We specialise in the fabrication and installation of heavy-duty GRP walkways, platforms, and access gantries for demanding industrial and commercial environments. Glass Reinforced Plastic is the ideal material, offering exceptional strength-to-weight ratio, inherent non-conductivity, and complete resistance to corrosion, rot, and harsh chemicals.

Our GRP walkways are equipped with a moulded-in anti-slip surface for maximum safety in all weather conditions and are designed to meet stringent health and safety standards. This results in a virtually maintenance-free, long-lasting access solution that ensures personnel safety and reduces lifetime costs.

GRP Ponds & Swimming Pools

Create a stunning, durable water feature with our bespoke GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic) ponds and pools. We design and fabricate seamless, one-piece shells that can be tailored to any shape, size, or depth, allowing for complete creative freedom to match your garden's landscape or architectural vision.

The non-porous, rigid GRP structure is incredibly strong, resistant to cracking from ground movement, and impervious to UV degradation and algae growth. This results in a virtually maintenance-free installation that provides a watertight, long-lasting solution, perfect for both naturalistic koi ponds and modern swimming pools.

General Glass Fibre (GRP) Fabrication

We specialise in versatile glass fibre (GRP) fabrication for a vast range of custom projects and industrial applications. This incredibly adaptable material allows us to create strong, lightweight, and complex shapes that are resistant to corrosion, weather, and impact, making it ideal for both functional and aesthetic purposes.

Our expertise covers everything from one-off prototypes and architectural features to durable industrial components and marine fittings. We handle the entire process, from mould making and lamination to finishing, delivering a high-quality, seamless product tailored to your exact specifications.

Architectural Mouldings

Add character, elegance, and a sense of history to any interior with our bespoke architectural mouldings. We specialise in the design and creation of classic cornicing, ceiling roses, skirting boards, and panel mouldings that can restore period charm or introduce timeless detail to a contemporary space.

Using traditional techniques and high-quality materials like plaster and fibrous plaster, we craft mouldings that are authentic in both proportion and detail. We offer both restoration of original features and the creation of new, custom designs to perfectly complement your property's architecture.

Expert Marine & Boat Repairs

We specialise in professional fibreglass (GRP) and hull repairs to restore your vessel's integrity and performance. Our expertise covers everything from fixing cosmetic gelcoat cracks and impact damage to addressing more serious structural issues, osmosis treatment, and deck delamination.

Using marine-grade materials and proven techniques, we ensure every repair is watertight, durable, and finished to a high standard. We can match existing gelcoat colours and textures, making the damage disappear and protecting your investment for years of safe sailing.

Custom Moulding & Fabrication Projects

We specialise in bringing complex designs to life through expert mouldmaking and fabrication for one-off projects or production runs. Using materials like GRP (fibreglass), composites, and resins, we can create virtually any shape, from sleek architectural features to durable industrial components.

Our end-to-end service manages your project from initial concept and pattern creation through to the final high-quality moulding. This approach is ideal for prototypes, specialist parts, or artistic installations, offering unparalleled design freedom and robust, lasting results.

Full Pattern Making for GRP Moulds

We specialise in the critical first stage of the GRP process: creating the master pattern. This is the exact replica of your final part, meticulously crafted by our experts from wood, clay, high-density foam, or from a CAD model using our CNC machinery. The precision of this pattern directly defines the quality of the subsequent mould and every part produced from it.

Our service is essential for both one-off custom projects and full production runs, ensuring dimensional accuracy and a perfect surface finish. We handle everything from the initial design consultation to the final sealed and polished pattern, ready for the mould-making process.

Benefits of GRP

Durability

GRP offers exceptional strength and impact resistance, lasting for decades with minimal maintenance.

Water Resistance

Perfect for wet environments, GRP is completely waterproof and resistant to rot and corrosion.

Design Flexibility

GRP can be molded into complex shapes and finished in a wide range of colors and textures.

Lightweight

Despite its strength, GRP is surprisingly lightweight, making installation easier and reducing structural loads.
