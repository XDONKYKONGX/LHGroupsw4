Bespoke Joinery

Transforming spaces with exquisite, custom-crafted woodwork designed uniquely for you and your home.

Master Craftsmanship in Wood

At LH Group SW Ltd, we believe that exceptional joinery is the heart of a beautiful home. Our master craftsmen combine traditional techniques with modern precision to create stunning, functional pieces that elevate your living space.

Each project is meticulously planned and crafted to your exact specifications, ensuring a perfect blend of form and function that reflects your personal style.

Standard Staircases

We offer a range of high-quality, pre-manufactured staircases to suit your project's needs and budget, with nationwide delivery available. Whether you are a trade professional looking for materials or a homeowner needing a complete solution, we provide reliable options for new builds, renovations, and replacements.

Choose from our 'Supply Only' service for direct delivery of the staircase kit to your site anywhere in the country, or our full 'Supply and Fit' service where our experts handle the entire installation, ensuring a perfect, safe, and building-regulations-compliant finish.

Bespoke Staircases

Transform the heart of your home with a stunning, one-of-a-kind staircase crafted to your exact specifications. We work with you to design a unique centrepiece that reflects your personal style, from sweeping helical designs to contemporary floating treads, using a range of premium materials like glass, steel, and hardwoods.

Our expert craftsmen blend traditional joinery with modern techniques to bring your vision to life. We manage the entire process, from initial concept and detailed CAD drawings to precise installation, ensuring a flawless finish that becomes the defining feature of your property.

Bespoke Windows & Doors

Enhance your property's character and performance with perfectly tailored windows and doors, crafted to your unique specifications. We design and create made-to-measure solutions for period properties, unusual openings, and contemporary architectural projects, using materials like timber, aluminium, and steel.

Our service combines expert craftsmanship with advanced manufacturing, offering a wide choice of styles, finishes, and glazing options. From elegant sash windows to striking pivot doors, we ensure a flawless fit that improves aesthetics, security, and energy efficiency for your home.

Bespoke Kitchen Design & Installation

We design and build beautiful, functional kitchens that are tailored to your space, style, and how you live. From initial concept to the final fitting, we work with you to create a truly custom kitchen that combines intelligent storage solutions with high-quality craftsmanship.

Our service includes everything from plumbing and electrical work to the installation of cabinets, worktops, and integrated appliances. We ensure a seamless, stress-free process, delivering a kitchen that is not only stunning but also a practical and durable heart of your home.

Bespoke Furniture & Joinery

Commission beautiful, one-of-a-kind furniture pieces designed and built exclusively for your space and style. We transform your ideas into functional art, creating everything from custom wardrobes and bookcases to unique tables and entertainment units that standard furniture cannot provide.

Our master craftsmen use the finest solid woods, veneers, and finishes to ensure exceptional quality and longevity. We manage the entire process from initial consultation and detailed drawings to precise installation, guaranteeing a perfect fit and a stunning centrepiece for any room.

Made-to-Measure Wardrobe Solutions

Reclaim your floor space and declutter your bedroom with a perfectly fitted wardrobe system. We design and install custom wardrobes for alcoves, awkward angles, and entire walls, creating a sleek, integrated appearance that free-standing furniture cannot match.

From initial design to final installation, we work with you to configure every shelf, rail, and drawer to suit your specific storage needs. Choose from a wide range of doors, from modern sliding styles to classic hinged designs, all finished to the highest standard.

Professional Shopfitting Services

We design and install commercial interiors that are engineered for customer flow, brand impact, and operational efficiency. Our comprehensive shopfitting solutions transform your retail space into a compelling environment that enhances the shopping experience and maximises your sales potential.

Our services include the installation of custom counters, shelving units, display systems, and lighting, all built to withstand high-traffic conditions. We manage the entire project from concept to completion, ensuring minimal disruption to your business and a finish that truly reflects your brand identity.

Professional Bar Fitting Services

We design and install fully functional, durable bars tailored to the high-demand environment of pubs, restaurants, and hotels. Our service focuses on creating efficient workspaces that enhance staff productivity and customer service, while reflecting your venue's unique brand and aesthetic.

Our comprehensive fitting includes the installation of the bar front, countertops, under-bar shelving, glasswashers, keg systems, and integrated refrigeration. We ensure all plumbing and electrical connections are professionally installed for a seamless, safe, and operational bar ready for business.

Expert General Carpentry

We provide skilled carpentry services for a wide range of projects, from structural framing and first-fix work to intricate finishing and custom details. Our expertise ensures that the essential wooden framework and features of your property are built with precision, strength, and quality craftsmanship.

Our services include floor and roof joisting, stud wall construction, door and window fitting, skirting boards, architraves, and decking. Whether for a new build, renovation, or repair, we deliver reliable and durable carpentry solutions for both residential and commercial clients.

Professional Decking Installation

Transform your garden into an extension of your living space with a bespoke, high-quality deck. We design and build durable, level decking areas that are perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, or relaxing, creating a seamless flow between your home and garden.

Using robust, weather-resistant materials like pressure-treated softwood, composite, or hardwoods, we ensure your deck is built to last. Our service includes solid foundations, safe railings, and a flawless finish, providing you with a beautiful, low-maintenance outdoor room for years to come.

Skilled Labour & Sitework Subcontracting

We provide reliable, skilled tradespeople and teams to support your construction projects and maintain critical deadlines. Our workforce is proficient in all phases of on-site work, from groundworks and structural framing to finishing trades, seamlessly integrating with your management structure.

Our flexible service includes labour-only or supplied labour for specific tasks, ensuring you have the right skills exactly when you need them. We are well-versed in site safety protocols and committed to delivering high-quality workmanship that meets your specifications and standards.
